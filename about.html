Our Story

Born from a love of beautiful detail.

Garnet Events & Production was founded in 2020 with a simple belief: a well-designed room can change how people feel. Built on years of hands-on experience with large-scale productions, it has grown into a full-service event design and production house, trusted for weddings, corporate events and milestone celebrations.

Today our studio brings together designers, producers, florists and technicians under one roof — so every event, however large, still feels handcrafted.

Meet The Founder

Hi, I'm Amit — I make events unforgettable.

Born from a deep passion for events, Amit Agarwal's journey began in 2018 at NIEM, where he built a strong foundation in event management. This was followed by hands-on experience with renowned productions such as Star Plus Dance and the Tata Steel Marathon, shaping his understanding of large-scale, high-pressure event execution.

After honing his skills through internships and freelance projects, Amit went on to establish Garnet Events & Production in 2020 with a clear vision — to create thoughtfully curated, seamlessly executed event experiences. Today, Garnet specialises in weddings, corporate events, birthday celebrations, intimate family gatherings and more — each delivered with attention to detail, creativity and professionalism.
