Our Story

Born from a love of beautiful detail.

Garnet Events began in 2013 with a simple belief: a well-designed room can change how people feel. What started as a small styling studio has grown into a full-service event design and production house, trusted for weddings, galas and milestone celebrations across the country.

Today our studio brings together designers, producers, florists and technicians under one roof — so every event, however large, still feels handcrafted.
