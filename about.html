Meet the founder

Amit Agarwal

“Hi, I'm Amit. I make events unforgettable.”

Amit's journey began in 2018 at NIEM, where he built a strong foundation in event management. Hands-on experience on renowned productions followed, among them Star Plus's Dance+ and the Tata Steel Marathon, and taught him how large, high-pressure events are actually run.

After further internships and freelance projects, he founded Garnet Events & Production in 2020 with a clear aim: thoughtfully planned, smoothly executed events.

Today Garnet plans weddings, corporate events, birthdays, family gatherings and more, each delivered with attention to detail, creativity and professionalism.
