About

A hands-on studio, founded in 2020.

Garnet started with one person's love of events and grew into a team that plans, designs and runs them end to end.

Meet the founder
The founder

“Hi, I'm Amit. I make events unforgettable.”

Amit Agarwal's journey began in 2018 at NIEM, where he built a strong foundation in event management. Hands-on experience on renowned productions followed — among them Star Plus's Dance+ and the Tata Steel Marathon — and taught him how large, high-pressure events are actually run.

After further internships and freelance projects, he founded Garnet Events & Production in 2020 with a clear aim: thoughtfully planned, smoothly executed events.

Today Garnet plans weddings, corporate events, birthdays, family gatherings and more — each delivered with attention to detail, creativity and professionalism.
