What We Do

Services Built Around Your Occasion

From intimate gatherings to nationwide productions — every service is tailored, never templated.

01 — Weddings

Ceremonies designed to feel timeless.

From mandap and altar design to reception styling and the final send-off, we craft every wedding moment with garnet-and-gold warmth, rich textures and considered lighting.

  • ✦ Full wedding design & art direction
  • ✦ Mandap, stage & floral installations
  • ✦ Multi-day function planning (sangeet, haldi, reception)
  • ✦ On-site production & vendor management

02 — Corporate & Galas

Productions built for impact.

Conferences, product launches, annual galas and award nights — engineered with precision staging, lighting and AV, without losing a premium feel.

  • ✦ Stage, set & branding design
  • ✦ Lighting, AV & technical production
  • ✦ Delegate experience & logistics
  • ✦ Nationwide multi-city rollout

03 — Social Celebrations

Milestones, made memorable.

Birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and intimate private gatherings — styled with the same care we bring to our largest productions.

  • ✦ Themed styling & decor concepts
  • ✦ Bespoke florals & tablescapes
  • ✦ Entertainment & guest experience curation
  • ✦ Full-service or styling-only packages

04 — Destination & Exhibitions

Scenic settings, seamlessly run.

Destination weddings, retreats and immersive brand exhibitions — we manage travel, local vendors and on-ground production so you simply arrive and celebrate.

  • ✦ Venue scouting & destination logistics
  • ✦ Guest travel & hospitality coordination
  • ✦ Immersive exhibition & brand experiences
  • ✦ On-ground local production teams
