Services

Plan your event
01

Weddings

From one ceremony to a week of functions, we design the look, book the right people and run the schedule, so your family can be part of the day instead of managing it.

  • Mandap, stage and décor design
  • Haldi, mehendi, sangeet and reception
  • Vendor booking and coordination
  • On-the-day management, start to finish
02

Corporate events

Conferences, product launches, annual days and award nights — produced with the polish of a show and the discipline of a schedule.

  • Stage, set and branding
  • Sound, light and AV production
  • Run-of-show and cue management
  • Guest arrival and logistics
03

Social celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and family get-togethers, styled with the same care we bring to a wedding.

  • Theme, décor and table styling
  • Entertainment and performers
  • Venue search, if you need one
  • Full planning or styling only
04

Destination events

Celebrations in another city: we scout the venue, line up local vendors and manage the logistics, so the move doesn't become your problem.

  • Venue scouting
  • Local vendors and on-ground team
  • Scheduling and logistics planning
  • Experience across eight cities, including Dubai
